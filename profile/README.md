### How we build

**Local.** Our tools run on your machine. Your agents talk to their own providers, the same way they do without us.

**Nothing is sent to us unless you say yes.**

**No Auralis account.** Nothing to sign up for, nothing resold.

**Free for people.** Personal use, freelancers doing client work, and teams of up to 10 people with revenue under €1 million use everything for free. Bigger companies buy a licence per seat. Schools, non-profits and open projects are free at any size, as long as the work isn't sold.

### Who we are

A small team of people and AI agents, based in Europe. We build every tool for ourselves first.
